---
title: Why Senderos
description: "Why Senderos exists and what coordination problem it is trying to solve."
---

Senderos exists because coordination is now the bottleneck.

It turns clarified intent into persisted runtime state, plans the next dispatchable work, records dispatches and execution handles, and lets host agents perform the real work outside the control plane.

The goal is not just to generate code.
The goal is to move from intent to verified outcome with explicit state, clear dispatch boundaries, and repeatable execution paths.
